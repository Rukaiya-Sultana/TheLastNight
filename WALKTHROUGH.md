# The Last Night — Walkthrough

## Controls

- **W / A / S / D** move; use the mouse to look.
- Hold **Shift** to run. Press **F** for the flashlight.
- Aim at an object until its prompt appears, then press **E** to examine or use it.
- Clue cards close with **E**. The library safe keypad accepts digits, **C** to clear, and **E** to submit; **Esc** closes it.
- **Esc** pauses. Follow the on-screen prompts when a door offers open/close.

## Main route

1. **Lobby:** Examine the faded letter on the coffee table. It points to the stopped clock. The east door is barred from the other side; leave it for now.
2. **Grand Hall:** Open the west doorway and enter the hall. Read the old letter on the writing desk, then examine the stopped clock near the library side. Its hands point to midnight.
3. **Library safe:** Follow the east-wall opening through the wide, open-leaf doorway into the library. The clock and side table are offset from this clear path. Examine the pulled red journal, the stopped clock, and the founder portrait/plate. Enter **127** (12 from midnight, 7 from the plate) on the safe. Use **E** to submit if it does not auto-submit. Examine the open safe again to take the mansion key. The garden door unlocks.
4. **Garden:** Return to the lobby and go through the east garden entrance. Examine the fountain plaque and statue. The statue reveals a hidden plate; examine it for the cellar location. Open the cellar door in the north-east annex and descend the stairs.
5. **Basement dials:** Turn each of the three dials until its crest lights. Starting from the initial position, turn the **left dial twice**, the **middle once**, and the **right three times**. When all three align, the stone panel opens.
6. **Secret room:** At the door exposed behind the panel, press **E** to release it and enter. Examine the crest and rotate its rings to match the carving: **outer once**, **middle three times**, **inner twice** from their initial positions. The cabinet unlocks. Open it and take the antique master key.
7. **Escape:** Return through the basement, garden, and mansion to the lobby. Read the brass plaque beside the front door. Turn the **moon twice**, **star once**, and **sun three times** from their initial positions (target marks 2, 1, 3). The front-door mechanism unlocks. Interact with the front door to swing it open, then walk outside through the entrance to trigger the ending.

## Optional investigation

In the library, the old brass key in the gap behind the red journal opens the study door. In the study, inspect the photograph, open and search the desk drawer, and read the hidden note. The mansion key opens the map closet, whose chart points to the garden. The garden plate, basement passage, and secret room also contain extra notes and photographs that expand the story but are not required for the main escape route.

If a door appears not to move after unlocking it, close its clue card with **E**, then interact with the door or its newly enabled close/open prompt once more. Door panels animate over a short moment.
